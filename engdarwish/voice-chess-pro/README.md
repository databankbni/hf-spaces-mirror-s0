# ♟ Voice Chess Pro

**Interactive chess with Stockfish AI — by Ahmed Darwish**

Play chess directly in your browser against the Stockfish engine (Easy / Medium / Hard), or challenge a friend in Player vs Player mode.

## How to play
- Type your move in the box: `e2e4`, `Nf3`, or `e2 to e4`
- Press **Move** or hit Enter
- Use **Undo** to take back moves
- Use **Flip Board** to switch perspective

Built with `python-chess` + `Gradio` + `Stockfish`.
