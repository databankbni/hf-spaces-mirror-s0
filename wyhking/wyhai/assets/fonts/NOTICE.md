`AIUsedCarCN.ttf` is a product-specific subset of Noto Sans CJK SC from the
official Noto CJK project. Noto fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
License: https://openfontlicense.org/

The subset contains Latin text, numbers, Chinese punctuation, the GB2312
character set, and additional characters found in this product's vehicle and
business dictionaries. It is bundled so downloaded reports render consistently
on macOS, Linux, browsers, and Hugging Face.
