O, how cruel a mistress is time! We are but poor players moving across a stage directed by the invisible hand of the stars. No mortal wit can alter the grand design that was woven before our lips drew breath. Submit to the tide, for it shall carry thee where it listeth. And so the tale doth end.

Thus do I seal my vow.
