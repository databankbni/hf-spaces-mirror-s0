Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how it works? We have answers.

How does the bot analyze my messages?
When you forward a message, our server extracts any phone numbers, links, and keywords. It queries our blacklist database first. If no exact match is found, Gemini 2.5 Flash reads the text, identifies red flags (like urgency, OTP requests, fake accounts), and structures a risk report.
Is my private data secure?
Yes. We value your privacy. The bot only reads messages you explicitly forward to it. It does not monitor your private conversations, calls, or media. We encrypt all logs and do not sell user data.
Why is there a limit of 5 checks/day on the free tier?
Running advanced AI servers (like Gemini) incurs compute and API charges. To keep the service open for everyone for free, we limit the free tier to 5 checks per day. Upgrading to the Premium plan for ₹199/month helps us cover API fees and continue developing features.
How do I add it to WhatsApp?
Just click the "Add to WhatsApp" button on this page. It will open a chat window on your WhatsApp. Send any message (e.g. "Hello") to activate the bot. Then you can start forwarding suspicious texts directly to it!
How does the bot scan media and images?
Simply forward any image, screenshot, or document to the bot. Our system downloads the attachment and forwards it to Gemini's vision engine to extract text and analyze visual red flags (like fake bank letterheads or threat seals). To optimize storage, we never save the raw image file—only a compressed text log description is stored.
Are my credit card or UPI details secure during upgrade?
Yes. We do not store or process card numbers or UPI PINs. All payments are securely routed and processed via Razorpay, India's leading PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway, protecting your details with banking-grade security.
Can I trust the risk level assessment?
Our engine combines pre-defined rules, real-time phishing URL lists, and Gemini's semantic logic. While the accuracy is extremely high (95%+), the report is a safety guideline. Always exercise personal caution and double-check with the official organization before making any payments.
How can I report a new scam to blacklist it globally?
Just send the word REPORT to our WhatsApp bot. The bot will guide you to submit the details (suspicious text, links, or screenshot images). Our AI will analyze your report and instantly blacklist confirmed threats globally to protect the entire community!
