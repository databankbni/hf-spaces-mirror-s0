## 💥 2. The $15,000/Month Pain Point (Enterprise Procurement Bounce)
B2B buyers research software differently than consumers. They have strict technical & compliance questions:

1. **Confusing Technical Docs & Pricing**: Enterprise buyers bounce off landing pages when API integration specs, competitor comparison tables, or security compliance rules (SOC2, GDPR, HIPAA) are hard to navigate.
2. **The 24-Hour "Request a Demo" Lag**: When an enterprise buyer fills out a static "Request a Demo" form, sales reps take 24 hours to email back. By then, the buyer has already signed up for a competitor who responded faster!
3. **Wasted Sales Rep Hours**: SDRs spend all day answering basic procurement questions instead of closing high-ACV deals.
