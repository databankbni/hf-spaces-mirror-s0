## 🛠️ 4. The Complete AI Solution Architecture
You deploy an **Installer Eligibility & Site Screener**:

1. **Eligibility Screener**: Asks 3 quick questions (*Homeownership status, average electric bill, roof age/condition*).
2. **Rebate & Tax Credit Explainer**: Answers questions about federal tax credits, state solar rebates, and financing options accurately.
3. **Triage Logic**: Filters out renters and non-eligible homes politely before wasting contractor time.
