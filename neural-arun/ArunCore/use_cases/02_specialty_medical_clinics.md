## 💥 2. The $20,000/Month Pain Point (Nighttime Patient Anxiety & Lost Surgeries)
High-ticket medical procedures involve intense patient hesitation and anxiety:

1. **Nighttime Anxiety & Price Doubts**: Patients research LASIK, IVF, or Hair Transplants at 10 PM. They have fear about pain, recovery time, and pricing tiers. If no one is available to answer, they close the tab and book a competitor the next morning.
2. **Front Desk Overload**: Clinic receptionists spend hours answering basic phone calls (*"How many days off do I need after hair transplant?", "Do you offer EMI / financing?"*) instead of booking consultation deposits.
3. **Lost $5,000 Procedures**: A single missed late-night inquiry means $5,000 to $10,000 in lost clinic revenue.

## 🛠️ 4. The Complete AI Solution Architecture
You deploy a zero-hallucination **Clinical Concierge**:
