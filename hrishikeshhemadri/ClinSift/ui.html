ClinSift/ real-world safety filter

Pre-clinical & clinical safety screening

Screen any drug against patient-level adverse-event signals.

ClinSift mines reactions, allergies, cross-reactivity and interactions from real clinical notes and returns one gate decision your pipeline can act on. Screen a known drug for observed evidence, or a novel compound by structure — the models run live.

How to read this. Signals marked observed come from documented reactions in real EHR notes — the differentiator over structure-only databases. Signals marked predicted come from the models (MoLFormer structural similarity + the SSI-DDI interaction model) and apply to compounds not in the record. Allergy cross-reactivity is patient-anchored and unavailable for novel compounds. Tuned for specificity: chemically unrelated co-occurrences are reported low, not as warnings.
