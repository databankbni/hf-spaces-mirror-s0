CODING IS SIMPLE, FUN, AND TRANSFORMATIVE  

• Stop guessing what to solve: Crackked automatically surfaces the exact next most accessible LeetCode* problem to build your pattern-matching intuition.  
• Learn organically: Bridge prerequisites and invent techniques on-the-fly before encountering them directly. Learning is the goal; speed is just a symptom.  
• Maintain momentum: Track your progress across difficulty tiers and watch your problem-solving skills grow. Keep up and build your mental sharpness via practice.  

⚠️ Note: Crackked is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to LeetCode®. This is an independent study companion.  

THE QUEST METHOD  

Crackked runs on a simple four-step loop:  
1. [Log] Mark problems as solved to feed your progress engine.  
2. [Setup] Fine-tune problem difficulty, premium filters, and topic biases to match your goals.  
3. [Path] Your individualized problem playlist, dynamically generated and calibrated for your level.  
4. [Progress] See where you stand across difficulty levels and track how your solve count grows over time.  

CORE FEATURES  

• Smart Playlists: Highly customizable problem sets that respond to your history and preferences.  
• Topic Bias & Exclusions: Total control over which topics you want to focus on or avoid.  
• Adaptive Milestones: Whether you're a seasoned developer, a competitive college coder, or just keeping your mind sharp, Crackked scales with you.  
• Theme Selection: Mellow vampire-friendly Dark mode or Classy ivory-based Light mode.  

100% PRIVATE & BLAZING FAST  

Offline-first: your logs and settings belong to you. No accounts required. Built with a lightweight, pure native Dart core that runs instantly on your device without relying on remote servers.  

100% free, forever. Unlock the full colour palette and support the developer through a one-time optional IAP.
