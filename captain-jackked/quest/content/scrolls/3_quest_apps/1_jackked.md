LIFTING IS SIMPLE, FUN, AND TRANSFORMATIVE  

• Say goodbye to spreadsheets: Jackked optimizes your next move for consistent strength and muscle gains.  
• Avoid burnout and boredom: Undertrain and you plateau. Overtrain and you burnout. Jackked keeps you in the sweet spot where gains are practically guaranteed.  
• Chase PRs: Hitting and sustaining personal records is the primary objective — Jackked prescribes the exact weights, sets, and reps to overcome strength plateaus.  

⚠️ Jackked generates training prescriptions, not medical ones. If you have health concerns, consult a professional before starting any new program.  

THE QUEST METHOD  

Jackked runs on a simple four-step loop:  
1. [Log] Track your lifts and earn Medals for new personal records.  
2. [Setup] Customize your athlete profile, movement patterns, and schedule.  
3. [Path] Your individualized roadmap: intelligent, actionable sets and reps calculated for today.  
4. [Progress] Visualize your strength levels against bodyweight-anchored global benchmarks.  

CORE FEATURES  

• Fast-Track Routines: Load highly effective pre-built programs with a single tap.  
• Intelligent Scaling: Automatically accounts for workout gaps, manages fatigue, and adjusts for light or deload days.  
• Rep Max Insights: Accurately estimates your 1-Rep Max and tracks your work capacity across lifts.  
• Theme Selection: Mellow vampire-friendly Dark mode or Classy ivory-based Light mode.  

100% PRIVATE & BLAZING FAST  

Offline-first: your logs and settings belong to you. No accounts required. Built with a lightweight, pure native Dart core that runs instantly on your device without relying on remote servers.
