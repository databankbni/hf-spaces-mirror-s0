# J.R. Kantor Research System

Search engine for J.R. Kantor's complete works on interbehavioral psychology.

## Corpus
- **20 Books** (1924-1984)
- **91 Articles** (1915-1987)
- **21 Reviews**

## Technology
- **FAISS** for vector search
- **Sentence Transformers** for embeddings
- **Gradio** for interface
- **23,801 indexed chunks**

## Usage
Enter your research question and the system will search through Kantor's complete works to find relevant passages with proper citations.
